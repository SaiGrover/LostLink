🤖 How matching works

After moderator approval, LostLink compares your report with reviewed reports using text, photographs and campus details. Similarity helps discovery; it is not proof of ownership.

Descriptions and photos provide the strongest signals. Location, category, date, brand and colour add context. Every suggested match includes its component scores.

A match never hands over your item automatically — a claim is always verified by a moderator first. 🔐
