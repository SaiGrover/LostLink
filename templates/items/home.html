Campus lost & found

Lost on one side.
Found on the other.
Linked in between.

LostLink replaces scattered group chats and notice boards with one searchable record. It compares every lost report with every found report, explains why two might be the same item, and leaves the final decision to people.

From missing
to handed back.

Four steps, each with a clear owner. The software does the searching; people make the decisions that matter.

  1. 01 · Report

    Describe it once

    Lost or found, with a campus zone, date, description and an optional photo. A moderator reviews it before it becomes searchable.

  2. 02 · Match

    Compared automatically

    Every approved report is scored against opposite reports. Strong candidates notify both people.

  3. 03 · Verify

    Prove it privately

    The claimant answers questions only the owner could know. A moderator reviews the answers and the evidence.

  4. 04 · Return

    Hand it over

    Contact details are shared only after approval. The case closes when the physical handover is confirmed.
