How matching works

Every approved lost report is compared with every approved found report on six signals, each with a fixed weight. If a report has no photo, the photo isn't counted against the match; its weight is shared among the other signals.

How an item gets back

A score only points two people at each other. Every step that decides who gets an item belongs to a person, and every decision is logged.

  1. 1

    Someone reports it

    The owner reports what they lost, or a finder what they picked up: what it looks like, where, when, and a photo if they have one.

  2. 2

    A moderator reviews it

    Reports are published only after review, so search stays free of duplicates and spam.

  3. 3

    LostLink finds the overlap

    Language and image models compare descriptions and photos, then weigh zone, category, date, brand and colour. Both people are notified, with the reasons.

  4. 4

    The owner answers private questions

    The finder or a moderator asks what only the owner would know. A moderator reads the answers and decides; nobody can approve their own claim.

  5. 5

    The handover is confirmed

    Contact details are shared only after approval. A moderator confirms the item changed hands and the report closes.
