The problem

Campus recovery today is scattered and unsafe

  • Scattered across class groups, notice boards and the security desk.
  • Unsearchable: posts get buried, with no filters for place, date or category.
  • Inconsistent: one wallet, many descriptions; photos often missing.
  • Unsafe: phone numbers posted publicly; anyone can say “that's mine”.
  • Unaccountable: nobody records who handed what to whom.

Our question

Can AI similarity narrow a noisy search while keeping people in control of who gets the item?

The solution

One searchable, auditable recovery desk

1 · ReportLost or found, zone, date, photo
2 · PublishLive at once; personal details checked first
3 · MatchSix signals, explained
4 · ClaimPrivate questions and evidence
5 · VerifyChecklist; item reserved
6 · Hand overOwner's one-time code
The central rule: claim approved ≠ item returned.

Student

Report, search, claim, answer, hand over with a code.

Moderator

Check sensitive reports and all claims (never their own), resolve disputes.

Administrator

Users and roles, categories and zones, analytics, exports.

AI matching engine

Six signals, weighted and explained

SignalWeight
Title + description0.30
Photograph (ResNet-50)0.30
Campus zone0.15
Category0.10
Date and time0.10
Brand / colour0.05

Score

Weighted sum of the signals that have evidence; the share compared is shown as evidence coverage.

Tiers

Strong > 0.85 Moderate 0.70–0.85 Weak < 0.70

Never an approval

A score ranks leads for people to check. It is not proof of ownership.

Ownership and handover

People verify. The owner's code confirms.

  • Evidence checklist before any approval.
  • One reserved claim per item, enforced by the database.
  • Disputes pause the handover; they never hand the item to someone else.
  • Independence: nobody decides their own case.

1  Owner shows a 6-digit code, once

2  Finder enters it: item returned

Salted HMAC

Never stored in plain text

10 minutes

Single use, then expires

5 tries

Then locked, rate limited

Private

Moderators never see it

Security and privacy

Every rule is enforced on the server

Campus identity

Email verification, campus-domain policy, rate-limited sign-in.

Private evidence

Expected answers hidden from claimants and the public.

Safe photos

EXIF and GPS removed; ID cards blurred for others.

Audit trail

Append-only log of every decision.

Web security

CSRF, HSTS, secure cookies, private media.

Safe production

Refuses weak secrets or a missing campus domain.

Limitations and next steps

What we can and cannot claim

We can say

  • The full workflow works end to end and is tested.
  • Scores are stable, explained and versioned.
  • Ownership and handover cannot be bypassed.

We cannot yet say

  • How accurate matching is on real campus reports.
  • That the tier thresholds are calibrated.
